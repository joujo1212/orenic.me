Adaptive Manipulator is a bench-scale robotics platform for testing control strategies under changing payloads. It pairs a simple mechanical design with a software loop that can retune motion profiles after calibration.

The interface emphasizes repeatable experiments: move plans are versioned, calibration snapshots are stored with the run, and each trial can be replayed against simulated or physical hardware.
